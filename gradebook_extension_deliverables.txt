1. Identify the type of data we are working with. Identify at least 2 categories the data could fit into and why. 
    In this project set, we are working with small data because the data contains a limited number of subjects
    and then their corresponding grades. Another category that the data could fit into is numerical data because
    the individual grades are numbers that are then used to calculate averages/GPAs. Another category the data could
    fit into is categorical data because the grades are organized into different subjects.

2. How do we handle our data in terms of the 5 Cs? In other words, what did we do to ____ (Collect/Cache/Clean
/Conclude/Convey) our data?:
    a. Collect: We collect our data by entering the subjects and their 
    corresponding grades into a CSV file. Users can also collect new data
    following the creation of the CSV file by adding grades
    through the main menu.
    b. Cache: We cache our data by storing the subjects and their corresponding
    grades in the CSV file and then loading them into a Python dictionary. 
    This allows the program to store and access the information from the CSV
    file when necessary.
    c. Clean: We clean our data from converting the grades from strings to
    integers/numerical values as well as making any inputs about the choices
    for the main menu/the subject inputs case-insensitive.
    d. Conclude: We analyze our data by different methods such as the 
    calculating the average grade for each subject, identifying which class
    is the user's best and worst, and also calculating their overall GPA. 
    e. Convey: We convey our results from our data through the Gradebook menu,
    which allows users to view their grades/averages/GPA. We also have a visual
    representation of their grades using Xs and *s.

What is left to be desired?
    Some other questions that we could ask is how a student's grades change
